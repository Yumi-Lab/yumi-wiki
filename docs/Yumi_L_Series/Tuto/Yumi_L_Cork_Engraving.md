# Cork Engraving

[![Yumi L-A4 laser engraver with Mario Kart cork coasters — Laser Engraving on Cork](/img/Yumi_L_Series/Tuto/yumi-l-a4-banner.jpg)](https://wanhao-europe.com/collections/yumi-graveur-laser/products/yumi-l-a4-laser-pour-gravure-et-decoupe-pre-commande)

Personalized coasters, engraved trivets, custom logos, or unique decorations — laser engraving on cork is becoming increasingly popular among creators and hobbyists. With the Yumi L-A4 laser engraver and LaserGRBL software, you can achieve precise, elegant results — even as a beginner.

## 1. Prepare Your File in LaserGRBL

1. Open your engraving file and check the lines per mm setting — it directly affects the final quality (adjust according to your material).

    ![LaserGRBL line-by-line quality setting and cork engraving results from 2000 to 10000 lines per mm](/img/Yumi_L_Series/Tuto/cork-lines-per-mm.jpg){ width="360" }

2. Enter your optimized settings and engraving dimensions.

    ![LaserGRBL target image dialog with engraving speed, laser power and size settings](/img/Yumi_L_Series/Tuto/cork-settings.jpg){ width="360" }

!!! tip "Origin point"
    The origin point, where the laser head starts moving, is represented by a yellow cross.
    Manually move the laser head to position it exactly where you want the engraving to begin.

    ![LaserGRBL preview showing the coaster design and the yellow origin cross](/img/Yumi_L_Series/Tuto/cork-origin-cross.jpg){ width="360" }

## 2. Position the Material

1. Place the cork disc on the edge of the honeycomb grid, and adjust the height using the guide.

    ![Yumi L-A4 laser head positioned over a cork disc on the honeycomb bed for height adjustment](/img/Yumi_L_Series/Tuto/cork-height-guide.jpg){ width="360" }

2. LaserGRBL can display the rectangular boundary of the engraving area, ensuring your design fits within the desired zone. This feature helps center your coaster perfectly. You can also turn on the laser at 3% power for fine positioning.

    ![LaserGRBL toolbar with the frame boundary and positioning buttons highlighted](/img/Yumi_L_Series/Tuto/cork-frame-boundary.jpg){ width="360" }

3. Activate the laser beam and position it at the base of the honeycomb.
   If you don't have one, use the button on the right of the planet icon and roughly center the laser head over the disc.

    ![Cork disc on the honeycomb bed aligned with the ruler origin](/img/Yumi_L_Series/Tuto/cork-laser-positioning.jpg){ width="360" }

4. Run a perimeter test to verify the alignment — the beam should skim the coaster's outer edge without going beyond it.
   If necessary, adjust the laser head until the alignment is perfect.

    ![Perimeter test: dashed engraving boundary skimming the cork disc edge](/img/Yumi_L_Series/Tuto/cork-perimeter-test.jpg){ width="360" }

5. Start the engraving.

    ![LaserGRBL with the coaster file loaded and the start button highlighted](/img/Yumi_L_Series/Tuto/cork-start-engraving.jpg){ width="360" }
